# Ring Nebula (NGC 6720, M57) – JWST NIRCam

Planetary nebula in the constellation Lyra, observed with NIRCam on the James Webb Space Telescope (Cycle 1 GO programme 1558, PI M. Barlow). Field of view ≈ 1.6 × 1.2 arcmin, north up, east left.

## Colour assignment (C_physical: chosen freely, grouped by physical origin, not by wavelength)

| Filter | Colour | Mainly traces |
|---|---|---|
| F162M | blue | mainly continuum, presumably free-free emission from ionised gas, plus hydrogen recombination lines |
| F212N | orange-red | mainly molecular hydrogen (H2) |
| F300M | yellow | continuum, H2 and hydrogen lines (mixed) |
| F335M | gold | continuum and H2, plus the 3.3 µm PAH band (mixed) |

Filter contents follow Wesson et al. 2024 (MNRAS; arXiv:2308.09027, Table 1), estimated for part of the bright ring. The recorded light is infrared; all colours are an assignment, not "true" colours.

## Processing

calibrated stage-3 images (`_i2d.fits`) from MAST → row destriping (local background model; approximate) → common grid 0.063″/px (`reproject_adaptive`) → background from sky boxes → asinh stretch per filter (each normalised to its own white point) → additive colour mixing → saturated star cores filled with white; no local adjustments. Generated by `astropipe.run` (astropipe 0.1.0) from `configs/ngc6720.toml`; all values in `params/ngc6720_run.json`.
