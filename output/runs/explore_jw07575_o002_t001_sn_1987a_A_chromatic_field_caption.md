## Processing

calibrated stage-3 images (`_i2d.fits`) from MAST → row destriping (sky background model; approximate) → common grid rotated to the field 0.063″/px (`reproject_adaptive`) → background from sky boxes → asinh stretch per filter (each normalised to its own white point) → additive colour mixing → saturated star cores filled with white, isolated bad pixels with the local median; no local adjustments → full observed field, not cropped. Generated by `astropipe.run` (astropipe 0.1.0) from `configs/explore/jw07575-o002_t001.toml`; all values in `params/explore_jw07575_o002_t001_sn_1987a_run.json`.
