# Southern Ring Nebula (NGC 3132) – JWST NIRCam

Planetary nebula in the constellation Vela, observed on 3 June 2022 with NIRCam on the James Webb Space Telescope (Early Release Observations, programme 2733). Field of view ≈ 1.7 × 1.7 arcmin, north up, east left.

## Colour assignment (C_physical: chosen freely, grouped by physical origin, not by wavelength)

| Filter | Colour | Mainly traces |
|---|---|---|
| F090W | yellow | broadband: stars, scattered light |
| F187N | light blue | ionised hydrogen (Paschen-α) |
| F212N | orange | molecular hydrogen (H2) |
| F356W | yellow-orange | broadband: stars, nebula |
| F405N | blue | ionised hydrogen (Brackett-α) |
| F470N | red | molecular hydrogen (H2) |

Line identifications after the JWST NIRCam filter documentation and Pontoppidan et al. 2022 (arXiv:2207.13067): F187N Paschen-α, F405N Brackett-α, F212N and F470N H2 lines; F090W/F356W broadband. The recorded light is infrared; all colours are an assignment, not "true" colours.

## Processing

calibrated stage-3 images (`_i2d.fits`) from MAST → common grid 0.063″/px (`reproject_adaptive`) → background from sky boxes → asinh stretch per filter (each normalised to its own white point) → additive colour mixing → saturated star cores filled with white, isolated bad pixels with the local median; no local adjustments. Generated by `astropipe.run` (astropipe 0.1.0) from `configs/ngc3132.toml`; all values in `params/ngc3132_run.json`.
