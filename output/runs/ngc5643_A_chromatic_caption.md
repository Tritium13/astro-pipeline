# NGC 5643 – JWST NIRCam

Barred spiral galaxy, JWST NIRCam imaging from programme 1685 ('Uncrowding the Cepheids for an Improved Determination of the Hubble Constant'), two observations (July 2023) combined. The thin black wedge right of the nucleus is sky not covered by either observation. Field of view ≈ 2.2 × 2.2 arcmin, north up, east left.

## Processing

calibrated stage-3 images (`_i2d.fits`) from MAST → row destriping (auto background model; approximate) → common grid 0.063″/px (`reproject_adaptive`) → background from sky boxes → asinh stretch per filter (each normalised to its own white point) → additive colour mixing → saturated star cores filled with white, isolated bad pixels with the local median; no local adjustments. Generated by `astropipe.run` (astropipe 0.1.0) from `configs/ngc5643.toml`; all values in `params/ngc5643_run.json`.
