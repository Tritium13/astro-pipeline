# NGC 602 – young star cluster in the Small Magellanic Cloud – JWST NIRCam

Young star cluster NGC 602 with its surrounding ring of gas and dust, on the outskirts of the Small Magellanic Cloud (a satellite galaxy of the Milky Way), roughly 200,000 light-years away. Most of the small orange ellipses are background galaxies far behind the cluster. JWST NIRCam imaging, programme 7035 ('Brown Dwarfs in NGC 602 in the SMC - An opportunity to characterize a substellar IMF at low metallicity'). Field of view ≈ 5.6 × 2.3 arcmin, rotated to the field: north points 9° counter-clockwise from up.

## Processing

calibrated stage-3 images (`_i2d.fits`) from MAST → common grid rotated to the field 0.063″/px (`reproject_adaptive`) → background from sky boxes → asinh stretch per filter (each normalised to its own white point) → additive colour mixing → saturated star cores filled with white, isolated bad pixels with the local median; no local adjustments → crop = largest rectangle inside the observed area. Generated by `astropipe.run` (astropipe 0.1.0) from `configs/explore/jw07035-o006_t001.toml`; all values in `params/explore_jw07035_o006_t001_ngc_602_run.json`.
