## Processing

calibrated stage-3 images (`_i2d.fits`) from MAST → row destriping (auto background model; approximate) → common grid 0.063″/px (`reproject_adaptive`) → background from sky boxes → asinh stretch per filter (each normalised to its own white point) → additive colour mixing → saturated star cores filled with white, isolated bad pixels with the local median; no local adjustments. Generated by `astropipe.run` (astropipe 0.1.0) from `configs/explore/jw07035-o006_t001.toml`; all values in `params/explore_jw07035_o006_t001_ngc_602_run.json`.
