# Star-forming pillar in the Carina Nebula – JWST NIRCam

Star-forming pillar in the South Pillars region of the Carina Nebula (NGC 3372), about 7,500 light-years away (assignment from the coordinates and the pillar pointing towards Eta Carinae, not an official designation). JWST NIRCam imaging, programme 12591 ('MIRI and NIRCam imaging of star forming pillar'). Field of view ≈ 2.1 × 5.6 arcmin, rotated to the field: north points 1° clockwise from up.

## Processing

calibrated stage-3 images (`_i2d.fits`) from MAST → common grid rotated to the field 0.063″/px (`reproject_adaptive`) → background from sky boxes → asinh stretch per filter (each normalised to its own white point) → additive colour mixing → saturated star cores filled with white, isolated bad pixels with the local median; no local adjustments → crop = largest rectangle inside the observed area. Generated by `astropipe.run` (astropipe 0.1.0) from `configs/explore/jw12591-o003_t001.toml`; all values in `params/explore_jw12591_o003_t001_star_forming_pillar_run.json`.
